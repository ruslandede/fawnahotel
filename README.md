# 🐾 Fawna Hotel  
Pet Boarding & Hotel Booking System

---

## 📌 Overview
Fawna Hotel is a pet boarding and hotel booking platform designed to help pet owners find and reserve safe and reliable accommodation for their pets.

Previously, pet boarding services were arranged manually through chat or direct contact, leading to limited availability visibility, booking conflicts, and lack of structured reservation management.

The system is designed to streamline booking processes, manage room availability, and improve overall service experience for pet owners.

---

## 🎯 Key Features
- 🏨 Pet Hotel Listing & Details  
- 📅 Booking & Reservation Management  
- 🐾 Pet Check-In & Check-Out Tracking  
- 🛏️ Room Availability Management  
- 📊 Booking Status & History
